# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Indian individuals and small businesses who need to collect one total through several fixed amount UPI QR codes, often while sharing or printing them for another person to scan.

## Product Purpose

Create standards based UPI payment QR codes entirely in the browser. Split₹1999 divides a total into chunks of at most ₹1,999, makes each QR downloadable, and explains what the tool can and cannot verify.

## Brand Personality

Direct, calm, and careful. The interface should feel like a precise desk tool.

## Anti-references

Avoid casino fintech styling, payment gateway claims, fake trust badges, decorative gradients, dark trading interfaces, and playful language around payment safety.

## Design Principles

- Put the payment task first.
- Explain material limitations at the moment they matter.
- Keep sensitive details in the browser.
- Make every generated payment easy to inspect and save.
- Use familiar controls and restrained motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion support, 44px touch targets, and plain English safety guidance.
