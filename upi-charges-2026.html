UPI charges from October 15: the ₹2,000 rule, without the WhatsApp scholarship.

a 0.4% MDR is coming to specified merchant UPI payments above ₹2,000. customers do not pay it directly. P2P stays free. yes, the internet has already discovered ₹1,999.

by a3ro.dev6 minute readprimary source: Ministry of Finance

the short answer

from 15 October 2026, specified person-to-merchant UPI payments above ₹2,000 attract a 0.4% merchant discount rate. the merchant side pays. ordinary customers and person-to-person transfers remain free.

01

what changed in the new UPI rules?

the Finance Ministry announced the framework on 15 September 2026. it ends the blanket zero-MDR treatment for a limited set of higher-value merchant payments. the new charge starts one month later, on 15 October.

for a covered ₹3,000 merchant payment, 0.4% is ₹12. for ₹50,000, it is ₹200. the standard charge is capped at ₹300, so the ceiling is reached at ₹75,000.

02

what UPI payments remain free?

  • all person-to-person transfers, regardless of amount
  • merchant payments up to ₹2,000
  • covered small merchants receiving up to ₹1 lakh a month through the P2PM category
  • the customer side; the government says merchants must not pass the MDR to buyers

03

the policy has categories, because of course it does

essential and thin-margin categories such as utilities get a flat ₹5 MDR above ₹2,000. capital-market payments use 0.02%, capped at ₹300. the standard 0.4% rate applies to the specified merchant transactions left outside those treatments.

none of this is a new fee charged to the person sending money to a friend. it is also not a government tax. the official position is that MDR revenue stays inside the payment ecosystem.

04

why is the UPI MDR controversial?

UPI grew while merchant payments were marketed as free. banks and payment apps say running the network costs money. merchants hear “0.4%” and see another cut from already thin margins. the notification says the charge should not reach customers; shopkeepers know that costs rarely sit quietly where a circular places them.

that tension is the whole case: the payment network needs funding, but a charge tied to one clean threshold creates an equally clean incentive to stay below it.

quick answers about UPI charges in 2026

will I pay 0.4% when I use UPI?

no. the government says customers cannot be charged. the MDR applies on the merchant side for specified P2M payments above ₹2,000.

are UPI transfers to friends and family still free?

yes. all P2P transfers remain free, irrespective of amount.

does the rule start immediately?

no. the announced effective date is 15 October 2026.

is this the old viral “18% GST on UPI” claim?

no. that 2025 claim was false. this is a separate 2026 merchant MDR framework.
