# Split₹1999 Design System

## Theme

Warm paper surfaces with ink black text and a restrained electric blue action color. Designed for bright, everyday environments where clarity matters more than atmosphere.

## Typography

Use the native system sans stack. Headings are compact and heavy; labels are medium weight; numeric amounts use tabular figures.

## Color

- Canvas: `oklch(0.975 0.008 88)`
- Surface: `oklch(0.995 0.004 88)`
- Ink: `oklch(0.19 0.02 255)`
- Muted: `oklch(0.47 0.02 255)`
- Line: `oklch(0.89 0.012 255)`
- Action: `oklch(0.56 0.19 257)`
- Success: `oklch(0.49 0.13 150)`
- Warning surface: `oklch(0.95 0.04 85)`

## Components

Controls use 12px corners and a 48px minimum height. QR outputs are plain white sheets with a crisp one pixel border. Motion is limited to 180ms state transitions and respects reduced motion.

## Layout

A slim top bar, concise introduction, and two column workspace on desktop. The form becomes a single column above results on narrow screens. Legal and explanatory content uses a 70ch measure.
