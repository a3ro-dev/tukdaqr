what actually happened

what changed on 15 September 2026?

the Finance Ministry announced a 0.4% MDR on specified person-to-merchant UPI payments above ₹2,000. person-to-person payments stay free. customers are not supposed to pay the MDR.

so splitting a payment saves 0.4%?

that is the joke, not a promise. small merchants receiving up to ₹1 lakh a month under the covered category remain exempt, and banks or acquiring providers decide how merchant rules are applied. TukdaQR offers QR codes, not a legal opinion or a guaranteed loophole.

why ₹1,999 instead of ₹2,000?

because a threshold appeared and the Indian internet immediately removed one rupee. the official framework says merchant payments up to ₹2,000 remain free, so ₹1,999 is satire with a safety margin.

where can I read the boring official version?

the Finance Ministry release explains who stays exempt, who pays MDR, and why customers should not be charged.

does this collect or track payments?

no. it creates payment instructions in QR form. check your bank or UPI app to confirm that each payment arrived.
