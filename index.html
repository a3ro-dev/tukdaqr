How it works

it does one thing.

  1. DivideThe total is split into ₹1,999 chunks plus the exact remainder.
  2. EncodeEach QR contains a standard upi://pay link with payee, amount, currency, and note.
  3. VerifyUPI apps handle authorization. Confirm every credit in your bank or UPI app.

the stuff that matters

straight answers

Why ₹1,999?

It is the splitting rule used by this utility. It is not a general UPI transaction limit, tax threshold, or guarantee of fee treatment.

Does this collect or track payments?

No. It creates payment instructions in QR form. Check your bank or UPI app to confirm that each payment was received.

Is my UPI ID sent anywhere?

No. Generation happens in your browser. This site has no account system, database, analytics, or payment server.
