Privacy

Effective 16 September 2026

Split1999 is designed to work without collecting personal information. The UPI ID, account holder name, amount, and note you enter are processed locally in your browser to create QR codes.

What we collect

We do not operate user accounts, a database, payment processing, advertising pixels, or analytics on this site. We do not receive the content entered into the generator.

Hosting data

Our hosting provider may process standard technical request data, such as IP address, user agent, requested page, and timestamps, to deliver and secure the site. Their handling is governed by their own privacy terms.

Downloads and local data

Downloaded QR images are saved to the location selected by your browser. Closing or refreshing the page clears generated results from the page. Browser history may still record that you visited this site.

Third parties

When a QR is scanned, the payer's chosen UPI app and financial institutions process the payment under their own terms. Split1999 does not receive transaction status or banking credentials.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the public GitHub repository. Do not include financial details in a public issue.
